# Contract, Market, and Free-Agent Overhaul

This plan extends `plans/7. season-scheduler-overhaul.md` and combines the
auction/season decisions with the contract-negotiation and free-agent-retention
changes agreed after that plan.

## 1. Goals

The implementation must:

- keep transfer auctions independent from season rollover;
- prevent rapid same-player resale with both a season rule and a time lockup;
- make every new contract negotiation an explicit player-demand decision;
- let every auction bidder choose the contract term they are accepting;
- charge signing and transfer fees at settlement, but charge salary only through
  normal payroll;
- include the remaining current-season salary flow in demand and financial
  calculations;
- automatically relist and eventually delete unclaimed free agents;
- keep all scheduler, persistence, AI, and retry paths on the same authoritative
  domain logic.

## 2. Contract Semantics

The contract-term selector is `1..maxContractSeasons`, currently `1..5`.

The selected term means additional complete seasons after the current season:

```text
contract horizon = current season + selected additional seasons
```

The current season is included even when only a small remainder is left. At the
next rollover one season is consumed, leaving the selected number of complete
seasons.

The contract duration should continue to use season units compatible with the
existing rollover model. The remaining current-season fraction is handled by
payroll, demand, and financial-commitment calculations rather than by treating
the partial season as a complete additional season.

For an auction that crosses rollover, the selected term remains fixed with the
first bid. The contract begins in the season in which the auction settles, so
the winner receives the settlement season plus the selected additional seasons.
The salary demand remains the value accepted when the bid was submitted.

## 3. Shared Salary-Demand Calculation

Create one authoritative salary-demand helper used by:

- human contract renewals;
- human free-agent bids;
- human club-to-club bids;
- AI renewals;
- AI free-agent bids;
- AI club-to-club bids.

Inputs must include:

- salary baseline;
- player overall;
- player age;
- selected additional seasons;
- remaining fraction of the current season.

The existing configurable raise model remains authoritative:

- `renewalMinRaise`;
- `renewalSkillRaiseWeight`;
- `renewalSkillExponent`;
- `renewalMaxRaise`;
- `renewalAgeCurve`.

Extend the existing equivalent fixed-salary/present-value calculation so the
partial current season is included. Let `f` be the remaining fraction of the
current season, `n` the selected additional seasons, `B` the salary baseline,
and `r` the calculated raise:

```text
current-season cost = B * f
future-season cost  = sum(B * (1 + r)^k) for k = 1..n

demand = round((current-season cost + future-season cost) / (f + n))
```

The result is a fixed per-season salary. The client never supplies an arbitrary
salary offer.

For free agents, `B` is the generated market salary baseline. For club-to-club
transfers and renewals, `B` is the player's current salary baseline.

The current-season fraction must use the authoritative multiplayer season clock
(`world.mp.seasonDayIndex`), not an ambiguous historical matchday value.

## 4. Human Renewals

The existing squad renewal flow remains player-selected, but becomes
take-it-or-leave-it:

- selecting a player opens the renewal dialog;
- selecting a term displays the exact salary demand;
- the player either accepts or cancels;
- there is no salary input field;
- the request submits only the selected term;
- the server recomputes the demand and applies exactly that salary;
- a salary below or above the demand is not accepted as an alternative offer.

Renewal application must:

- settle accrued payroll under the old contract;
- reset the payroll period at the renewal point;
- set the new salary to the exact demand;
- set the contract to current season plus the selected term;
- recalculate the release clause;
- preserve the existing morale effect;
- use the shared financial decision boundary and warning behavior.

Renewal should remain unavailable for youth players, loaned players, and any
player with an active market listing. The route and UI must enforce the same
eligibility rule.

AI renewals must accept the exact demand or decline. They must not submit an
arbitrary salary above or below the demand.

## 5. Bid-Specific Contract Terms

Each bid must contain two independent commitments:

- private maximum transfer/signing fee;
- accepted contract term and exact salary demand for that term.

Extend `MarketBid` with bidder-specific contract data, including:

- selected additional seasons;
- accepted salary per season;
- the calculation snapshot or demand value needed for audit and deterministic
  settlement.

On the first bid, the server must:

- validate the selected term against `maxContractSeasons`;
- calculate the demand from the authoritative baseline and current-season
  fraction;
- store the exact accepted salary;
- validate the private fee maximum using the existing immediate-cash and market
  rules;
- reserve only the fee maximum when the bidder leads.

Subsequent submissions for the same bidder/listing may only increase the private
fee maximum. They may not change the selected term or salary.

The contract term and salary remain private bidder data. Public listing views
may expose the player's demand options, but never competing private maximums or
another bidder's selected terms.

The auction winner is still determined by the private fee maximum and existing
tie-priority/proxy rules. The winner's stored contract terms are applied at
settlement.

## 6. Transfer Auction Settlement

Club-to-club settlement must:

- charge only the final transfer fee at settlement;
- not charge the buyer salary immediately;
- apply the winning bidder's accepted salary;
- apply the winning bidder's selected contract term;
- reset the player's payroll period at the transfer point;
- let normal future payroll deduct the new salary;
- release all fee reservations;
- record the accepted contract terms in immutable transaction history.

The buyer no longer inherits the seller's salary or remaining contract.

The seller's accrued salary must still be settled under the existing ownership
rules before ownership moves. That is separate from the buyer's future payroll.

## 7. Free-Agent Settlement

Free-agent listings no longer define one universal salary and contract duration
for all bidders.

The listing must retain a generated salary baseline, frozen at the initial
unclaimed transition and preserved through relists. Each bidder then selects a
term and accepts the corresponding demand from that baseline.

Free-agent settlement must:

- charge only the winning signing fee;
- not charge salary immediately;
- apply the winning bidder's accepted salary;
- apply the winning bidder's selected term;
- reset payroll at settlement;
- release all fee reservations;
- record the accepted contract terms in immutable history.

The old listing-level `demandedSalary` and `demandedContractDays` values should
be removed from the active domain model or retained only as nullable legacy
columns for completed historical rows.

## 8. Financial Commitments

Fee reservations and salary commitments remain separate.

- A leading bidder reserves its private fee maximum.
- A leading bidder's accepted salary is included in contingent salary
  commitments.
- The contingent salary commitment covers the remaining current-season salary
  horizon used by the existing financial-control rules.
- Future contract seasons are not immediately deducted or charged in cash.
- Outbidding releases both the fee reservation and the contingent salary
  commitment for the displaced bidder.
- After rollover, surviving leading bids continue to affect the next season's
  financial cushion and are recalculated against the new current-season
  horizon.
- Human clubs may continue with a negative cushion, with warnings.
- AI clubs must not select terms that make their financial cushion unsafe.

Update `contingentSalaryFromLeadingBids`, the shared commitment calculator, and
AI affordability logic to read the leading `MarketBid` salary rather than a
listing-level salary.

No salary cash deduction occurs when an auction settles. Payroll remains the
only salary deduction path.

## 9. Transfer Lockup and Season Rules

Add a configurable transfer lockup under `MARKET_CONFIG.transferAuction`:

```ts
transferLockupDays: 15
```

The transfer-listing validation must reject a player when either condition is
true:

- the player's last permanent club-to-club acquisition occurred in the current
  season;
- fewer than the configured lockup days have elapsed since the last permanent
  acquisition.

The time lockup should use the persisted acquisition timestamp and real elapsed
time, matching real-time auction deadlines. Free-agent signings remain exempt
from the club-to-club resale restriction.

Apply the same validation to:

- auction creation;
- auction preview;
- human and AI listing paths.

## 10. Auction Rollover Independence

Transfer auctions are independent of the season calendar:

- active auctions may cross season rollover;
- deadlines remain real-time deadlines;
- bids and reservations remain active;
- an auction is not cancelled merely because the season changes;
- settlement history uses the season and `seasonDayIndex` at settlement.

Remove the transfer-auction rollover rejection and do not invoke the old
`reconcileListingsAtRollover` behavior for ordinary rollover.

Preserve cancellation when the seller or player is actually removed, such as
ephemeral filler-club cleanup.

Season rollover must also stop clearing `player.onSale` for players with an
active transfer listing. The flag must reflect active listing state after
rollover.

## 11. Transaction Day Rename

Rename `PlayerMarketTransaction.matchday` to `seasonDayIndex`.

The value is the zero-based game-day index within the season, not necessarily a
day on which a match was played.

Update:

- `backend/src/game/types.ts`;
- `backend/prisma/schema.prisma`;
- market, free-agent, and finance transaction creation;
- save serialization and loading;
- recent-trade round calculations;
- tests and fixtures.

The Prisma migration must preserve existing values. Add contract term and
salary fields to completed market history so later reports can explain the
contract created by a settlement. Loan history uses null contract fields.

## 12. Free-Agent Retention Configuration

Add the following to `backend/config/game.config.jsonc`:

```jsonc
// Real-time days an unclaimed free agent remains available across relists.
"freeAgentRetentionDays": 30
```

Add schema validation requiring a positive integer and include `30` in
`DEFAULT_GAME_CONFIG`. A schema default should keep older/custom config files
loadable.

## 13. Persisted Unclaimed State

Add `unclaimedSince` to `FreeAgentListing`.

- New initial listings set it to the transition timestamp.
- Relists preserve the original value.
- Legacy rows with no value fall back to the original listing `createdAt`.
- The fallback is persisted on the next save.
- The value must survive server restarts and season rollover.

The initial free-agent transition must create a listing for:

- manual senior-player release;
- contract expiry;
- financial-intervention liquidation.

Youth dismissal remains an academy operation and must not create an ineligible
free-agent listing.

## 14. Shared Free-Agent Due Processing

Create one authoritative due-listing operation used by both the durable
scheduler and `auctionProcessor`.

For a due active listing:

- with a valid bid, settle normally using the winner's bidder-specific contract
  terms;
- without a bid and before retention expiry, close and relist while preserving
  `unclaimedSince` and the salary baseline;
- without a bid at or after retention expiry, delete the player and all related
  live free-agent market state.

Deletion must remove:

- the player;
- all related free-agent listings;
- all related free-agent bids;
- all related free-agent reservations;
- all related free-agent AI evaluations.

Deletion must not remove immutable `PlayerMarketTransaction` history.

The retention boundary is evaluated when the due listing is processed. With the
existing one-day free-agent listing duration, deletion may occur at the next
listing deadline after the exact retention timestamp.

## 15. Scheduler Integration

Production uses the durable scheduler as the authoritative auction path.

Update the free-agent `AUCTION_END` handler to call the shared due-listing
operation instead of only calling `settleFreeAgentListing`.

When a relist is created:

- schedule its new `AUCTION_END` event immediately;
- use an idempotent key containing the new listing identity/deadline;
- retain season-event materialization as a repair path.

Update `auctionProcessor` to use the same operation rather than separately
settling and relisting listings.

When a player is deleted:

- cancel pending or failed free-agent auction events for every related listing;
- exclude the currently executing event from premature cancellation;
- ensure no materializer can recreate events for deleted listings;
- persist a world with no dangling market state.

## 16. Auction and Contract Data Migration

During deployment, clear existing active auctions as permitted:

- cancel active transfer listings;
- release their reservations;
- remove their active bids and AI evaluations;
- cancel pending auction events;
- clear seller/player `onSale` state.

For active free-agent listings:

- release reservations;
- remove active bids and AI evaluations;
- cancel pending events;
- preserve the oldest known `unclaimedSince` and salary baseline;
- recreate one valid active listing per eligible unowned player.

Completed auctions and immutable transaction history remain intact. Legacy
completed free-agent listing terms may remain in nullable compatibility columns,
but all new active listings use the bidder-specific contract model.

Existing active auction rows do not need bidder-term migration because they are
cleared during deployment.

## 17. API Changes

Renewal endpoint:

- accept only the selected term;
- calculate the exact salary server-side;
- remove the salary offer field.

Bid endpoints:

- require the selected additional seasons;
- calculate and store the exact salary demand server-side;
- continue accepting the private fee maximum;
- reject term changes after the first bid;
- return the selected term/demand in the bidder's private response.

Listing and contract-preview responses must expose salary demands for each
available term without exposing competing private maximums or contract choices.

## 18. Frontend Changes

Renewal dialog:

- remove the editable salary input;
- display the exact demand for each term;
- let the user accept or cancel;
- label the selector as additional seasons after the current season.

Free-agent and transfer bid dialogs:

- display demand options by term;
- let the bidder select one term;
- submit the term and private fee maximum;
- show that the salary will be paid through normal payroll, not immediately;
- use the selected salary in financial warnings.

Contract displays must distinguish the current-season portion from the
additional full-season term rather than rounding a partial horizon into a
misleading number.
