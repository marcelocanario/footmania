# Multiplayer League Redesign — Full Implementation Plan

## 1. Objective  [x]

Convert the existing single-player/AI league structure into a persistent asynchronous multiplayer football pyramid with:

* 8 clubs per division.
* 14 league matches per club per season.
* Double round-robin schedule.
* One match every other real-world day.
* One synchronized global season per calendar month.
* Unlimited pyramid depth.
* Human teams concentrated together whenever possible.
* AI clubs used only as filler at the bottom edge of the pyramid.
* Automatic promotion/relegation between tiers.
* Mid-season human entry up to a configurable cutoff.
* A provisional/interseason system for players joining after the cutoff.
* End-of-season removal of abandoned clubs.
* Persistent clubs that retain their roster, finances and progression when returning.
* Minute-level background processing for systems such as auctions.

---

# 2. Core Terminology  [x]

## 2.1 Season

A season corresponds to one calendar month.

Example:

```text
2026-01
2026-02
2026-03
```

All divisions operate on the same global season.

A season:

* starts on the first day of the month;
* contains 14 league rounds;
* schedules one round every other day;
* may finish several days before the end of the month;
* uses the remaining days as an interseason period;
* resets standings when the next season begins.

---

## 2.2 Tier

The competitive level within the pyramid.

```text
Tier 1
Tier 2
Tier 3
Tier 4
...
```

The tier is persistent and competitively meaningful.

Individual division groups inside a tier are **not persistent**.

---

## 2.3 Division

An 8-team competition group within a tier.

Naming:

```text
1
2.1
2.2
3.1
3.2
3.3
3.4
...
```

Maximum theoretical divisions per tier:

```text
Tier 1: 1
Tier 2: 2
Tier 3: 4
Tier 4: 8
Tier N: 2^(N-1)
```

However, divisions are created **only when humans require them**.

Empty divisions are deleted.

Example with 16 humans:

```text
Division 1   = 8 humans
Division 2.1 = 8 humans
Division 2.2 = does not exist
```

Do not create empty pyramid branches preemptively.

---

# 3. Global Time Model  [x]

The multiplayer game must no longer depend on user actions to advance time.

Introduce a server-authoritative global clock.

At minimum, support:

```text
MONTH
DAY
HOUR
MINUTE
```

Different systems operate at different resolutions.

### Monthly

* season rollover;
* promotion;
* relegation;
* division reconstruction;
* standings reset;
* new season budgets;
* new schedules.

### Daily

* player age/development processing where appropriate;
* game-day state;
* inactivity calculation;
* other club simulation processes.

### Match schedule

* one league round every two days.

### Minute-level ticker

Required for:

* auction start/end;
* bid deadlines;
* future marketplace events;
* other time-sensitive multiplayer systems.

Do not make the minute ticker responsible for expensive global calculations every minute.

Prefer timestamp-based event scheduling:

```text
nextExecutionAt
```

and query/process only due events.

---

# 4. Season Calendar  [x]

## 4.1 Schedule

Each 8-team division plays:

```text
7 opponents × home/away = 14 matches per club
```

Generate 14 rounds using a deterministic round-robin scheduler.

Recommended calendar:

```text
Day 1   Round 1
Day 3   Round 2
Day 5   Round 3
...
Day 27  Round 14
```

This leaves several days in longer months as interseason.

The exact kickoff time can be configured separately.

---

## 4.2 Season States

Recommended:

```text
PREPARATION
ACTIVE
INTERSEASON
ROLLOVER
```

### PREPARATION

Short period immediately before season initialization if technically required.

### ACTIVE

League matches are being played.

### INTERSEASON

All rounds have finished but the next calendar month has not started.

Players may:

* manage squads;
* trade;
* participate in auctions;
* negotiate contracts;
* configure tactics;
* train;
* prepare for next season.

### ROLLOVER

Short server-side transactional state during promotion/relegation and division reconstruction.

Users should not be allowed to perform operations that would conflict with league reassignment during this state.

---

# 5. Pyramid Structure  [x]

The competition follows a binary-tree topology.

```text
                 Division 1
                 /        \
             2.1            2.2
            /   \          /   \
          3.1   3.2      3.3   3.4
```

Each non-bottom division conceptually has:

* two child divisions;
* two relegation slots;
* up to two corresponding promotion slots.

However, nonexistent child divisions are not materialized.

---

# 6. Human Density Rule  [x]

The highest priority when creating divisions is:

> Maximize human-vs-human competition.

Always fill one division before creating another.

Example with 13 humans requiring Tier 3:

```text
3.1 = 8 humans
3.2 = 5 humans + 3 AI
3.3 = nonexistent
3.4 = nonexistent
```

Never distribute them as:

```text
3.1 = 4 humans
3.2 = 3 humans
3.3 = 3 humans
3.4 = 3 humans
```

Human density takes precedence over:

* timezone;
* geography;
* competitive balancing.

Timezone optimization occurs only after determining the minimum required number of divisions.

---

# 7. Initial Game State  [x]

The game launches with:

```text
Division 1
8 AI clubs
```

There are no lower divisions initially.

As humans join, they replace AI clubs according to the joining rules below.

Eventually Division 1 becomes entirely human.

After Division 1 is full, Division 2.1 is created when another eligible human needs placement.

---

# 8. New Human Club Creation  [x]

A player's club is persistent and independent of the league slot it occupies.

Club creation includes:

* club ID;
* owner/user ID;
* club name;
* colors;
* crest;
* initial roster;
* initial finances;
* facilities;
* contracts;
* tactics;
* timezone;
* progression;
* creation timestamp.

Do **not** use the replaced AI club as the human club's persistent record.

Instead:

```text
competition slot → old AI club
competition slot → new human club
```

The competition history remains attached to the slot/season record as necessary.

---

# 9. Account Timezone  [x]

Capture the player's IANA timezone during onboarding.

Example:

```text
America/Moncton
Europe/London
America/Sao_Paulo
Asia/Tokyo
```

Do not store only:

```text
UTC-3
```

because UTC offsets change with DST.

Allow the player to update timezone later.

Timezone changes affect division clustering only at the next season rollover.

Never move a club between divisions during a season because its timezone changed.

# 10. Mid-Season Join Threshold  [x]

Add configurable setting:

```text
JOIN_THRESHOLD_PERCENT = 0.50
```

With 14 matches:

```text
floor(14 × 0.50) = 7
```

Therefore, once 7 rounds have elapsed, new clubs may no longer enter an active division.

Use **completed global rounds**, not number of matches personally played.

Recommended calculation:

```ts
joinLockRound = Math.floor(totalRounds * joinThresholdPercent)
```

Joining permitted while:

```text
completedRounds < joinLockRound
```

or equivalent explicit semantics chosen once and covered by tests.

Avoid ambiguous `<` versus `<=` behavior.

---

# 11. Joining Before the Lock  [x]

If a human joins while current-season placement remains open:

1. Find the current lowest active tier.
2. Find the first division in that tier containing AI filler.
3. Select the **highest-ranked replaceable AI club**.
4. Replace it with the human club.
5. Preserve the AI slot's current-season competition record.
6. Human controls the club from that point onward.

If there is no available AI slot:

1. determine whether another division may exist at that tier;
2. create the next division;
3. simulate it up to the current global round;
4. place the new human into the highest-ranked AI slot.

---

# 12. What a Human Inherits From a Replaced AI  [x]

The human inherits **only current-season competition state**.

Inherited:

* league points;
* wins;
* draws;
* losses;
* goals scored;
* goals conceded;
* goal difference;
* matches played;
* already-played fixture results;
* position implied by those values.

Not inherited:

* players;
* money;
* salaries;
* contracts;
* facilities;
* club reputation;
* player statistics;
* player history;
* club progression;
* AI identity;
* AI name;
* AI crest;
* AI colors.

The human club identity replaces the AI identity immediately.

Historical fixture result remains valid.

Example:

```text
Round 2:
AI United 2–0 Club B
```

AI United is later replaced by:

```text
Marcelo FC
```

The Round 2 result remains part of the competition record.

The UI may now display the club's current identity, but the underlying immutable fixture result remains unchanged.

---

# 13. Creating a Division Mid-Season  [x]

A newly required division must not start at Round 1 while everyone else is at Round 6.

Procedure:

1. Create 8 temporary AI clubs.
2. Generate the complete 14-round schedule.
3. Determine the current global completed round.
4. Simulate all elapsed rounds.
5. Store:

   * fixture results;
   * points;
   * W/D/L;
   * GF/GA;
   * standings.
6. Select the highest-ranked AI.
7. Replace it with the joining human.
8. Continue normally from the next scheduled round.

This produces a division synchronized with every other division.

---

# 14. Joining After the Lock  [x]

Once the join threshold has passed:

* the user can still create their club;
* the club is not assigned to a league;
* the club enters a `PROVISIONAL` state;
* the club waits for next month's season.

Recommended club competition states:

```text
ACTIVE
PROVISIONAL
DORMANT
```

Possible separate account state:

```text
ACTIVE
INACTIVE
ABANDONED
```

Do not conflate competition participation with user activity.

---

# 15. Provisional Club Gameplay  [x]

Players waiting for the next season must still have meaningful gameplay.

Allow them to:

* create/customize the club;
* generate their initial roster;
* configure lineups;
* configure tactics;
* configure training;
* inspect player attributes;
* scout;
* negotiate contracts;
* participate in auctions;
* sign free agents;
* manage squad composition;
* manage facilities where appropriate;
* view current competitions;
* prepare transfer targets;
* play non-persistent practice/friendly simulations.

Do not allow practice matches to generate:

* league points;
* competitive statistics;
* prize money;
* permanent match XP;
* persistent form bonuses;
* injuries;
* disciplinary consequences.

This prevents waiting players from farming progression.

# 16. Provisional Budget  [x]

A provisional club receives its **next-season allocation immediately**.

Example:

```text
Club created August 20
→ receives September budget immediately

September 1
→ receives no additional September budget
```

Store budget issuance by season:

```text
club_budget_allocation
- clubId
- seasonId
- amount
- issuedAt
- reason
```

Enforce uniqueness:

```text
UNIQUE(clubId, seasonId, allocationType)
```

This prevents duplicate allocations regardless of race conditions or retries.

---

# 17. Pre-Lock Budget  [x]

A club entering the active season should not receive a full month's economic benefit after half the season has already passed.

Use a prorated current-season allocation.

Example formula:

```text
proratedBudget =
    normalSeasonBudget
    × remainingRounds
    / totalRounds
```

Then:

```text
next month → normal full allocation
```

Make this configurable if the existing economic model uses something other than a direct season allocation.

## 17A. Seasonal Budget Calculation by Tier  [x]

The seasonal budget must be derived from the game's existing economy rather than using an arbitrary hardcoded amount.

### First-Division Budget Initialization

Add a persistent setting:

```ts
FIRST_DIVISION_SEASON_BUDGET
```

This value becomes the authoritative Tier 1 seasonal budget once initialized and must be editable through the normal game/admin settings.

On first migration/startup, if `FIRST_DIVISION_SEASON_BUDGET` is not yet defined, calculate an initial value from the existing economy.

The initialization logic must inspect the relevant existing game settings and economic formulas, including where applicable:

* normal senior squad size;
* expected starting squad quality for a Division 1 club;
* existing player market-value calculation;
* existing salary calculation;
* expected seasonal wage bill;
* initial/new-club budget settings;
* typical transfer/auction costs;
* expected number of meaningful player acquisitions per season;
* recurring club expenses;
* existing prize/income sources;
* any existing economic constants used to determine club purchasing power.

Do **not** derive the value simply from the richest existing club or current save-game balances, since these may be distorted by historical gameplay.

### Economic Rationale

The calculated Tier 1 budget should represent the amount of new capital a healthy top-division club needs each season to:

1. pay its normal operating costs;
2. maintain a competitive squad;
3. make approximately **1–2 meaningful first-team acquisitions per season** without being able to replace most of its squad every month;
4. retain some room for cheaper/developmental signings;
5. require trade-offs for elite/star players rather than making them routine purchases.

A recommended derivation is:

```ts
expectedSeasonWages =
    expectedAveragePlayerSalary
    * expectedSeniorSquadSize;

expectedTransferSpend =
    expectedMeaningfulSigningsPerSeason
    * expectedFirstTeamPlayerMarketValue;

expectedRecurringCosts =
    calculateExpectedRecurringClubCosts();

rawTier1Budget =
    expectedSeasonWages
    + expectedTransferSpend
    + expectedRecurringCosts;
```

If salaries or recurring costs are handled through separate income streams and should **not** be funded directly by the seasonal allocation, exclude them from `rawTier1Budget` rather than counting them twice.

The coding agent must inspect the current economy before choosing which components belong in the calculation.

### Affordability Sanity Check

Before persisting the calculated value, validate it against representative player values.

For example, determine market values for approximately:

* an average Division 1 starter;
* a strong Division 1 starter;
* a star/elite Division 1 player.

The initialized budget should normally satisfy:

```text
average starter:
comfortably affordable

strong starter:
meaningful but reasonable purchase

elite/star player:
major expenditure requiring meaningful budget allocation
```

If the calculated budget would allow a club to purchase many elite players every season, it is too high.

If a normal club cannot reasonably improve one or two starting positions, it is too low.

This sanity check should influence initialization only; it should **not dynamically recalculate budgets after the game is live**.

### Persist the Result

After deriving the initial value:

```ts
if (settings.FIRST_DIVISION_SEASON_BUDGET == null) {
    settings.FIRST_DIVISION_SEASON_BUDGET =
        calculateInitialFirstDivisionSeasonBudget();

    saveSettings();
}
```

After this initial calculation, **never automatically recalculate it because player values, salaries, or other economic variables change**.

`FIRST_DIVISION_SEASON_BUDGET` becomes a balancing parameter controlled through settings.

This is important because otherwise changing the player-value formula could unexpectedly change the entire league economy.

---

### Lower-Tier Budget Curve

All lower-tier budgets derive from the configured Tier 1 budget.

Use an exponential decay toward a configurable minimum fraction of the Tier 1 budget:

```ts
tierBudget =
    minimumBudget +
    (firstDivisionBudget - minimumBudget)
    * Math.exp(-decayRate * (tier - 1));
```

Where:

```ts
firstDivisionBudget =
    settings.FIRST_DIVISION_SEASON_BUDGET;

minimumBudget =
    firstDivisionBudget
    * settings.MINIMUM_TIER_BUDGET_RATIO;

decayRate =
    settings.TIER_BUDGET_DECAY_RATE;
```

Add configurable settings:

```ts
FIRST_DIVISION_SEASON_BUDGET
MINIMUM_TIER_BUDGET_RATIO
TIER_BUDGET_DECAY_RATE
```

The intent is:

```text
Tier 1 → largest budget difference
Tier 2 → noticeably lower
Tier 3 → somewhat lower
Tier 4 → smaller difference
Tier 5+ → differences progressively shrink
Very low tiers → approach, but never fall below, the configured floor
```

This is preferable to linear decay because the pyramid has unlimited depth and a linear formula would eventually generate excessively small or negative budgets.

### Performance Modifier

After determining the tier budget, a small performance modifier may be applied based on the club's previous-season finish:

```ts
seasonBudget =
    tierBudget * performanceModifier;
```

Keep this modifier intentionally small so successful clubs do not compound their advantage excessively.

Promotion/relegation must use the club's **new season tier** when determining the next allocation.

### Budget Is an Allocation, Not a Balance Reset

The calculated seasonal budget is added to the club's existing finances:

```ts
club.balance += seasonBudget;
```

Do not reset the club balance to the calculated amount.

All seasonal allocations must continue to use the existing one-allocation-per-club-per-season protections described in the multiplayer plan.


# 18. Provisional Contract Freeze  [x]

This is critical.

While a club is `PROVISIONAL`, player contracts must **not consume contract time**.

Example:

Player signs:

```text
2-season contract
```

Club remains provisional for 12 days.

At the next season start:

```text
contract remaining = 2 seasons
```

It must not become:

```text
1 season + 18 days
```

Contract duration should therefore be based primarily on **participating seasons**, not wall-clock elapsed time.

Recommended representation:

```ts
contractSeasonsRemaining
```

rather than:

```ts
contractExpiresAt
```

If partial-season contract semantics exist, explicitly exclude provisional calendar time.

---

# 19. Provisional Salary Freeze  [x]

While `PROVISIONAL`:

```text
salary expense = 0
```

No salary processing should occur.

This applies to players acquired:

* during initial squad generation;
* through auctions;
* from free agency;
* through other allowed market mechanisms.

Salary begins once the club enters an active competition season.

---

# 20. Provisional Player Aging and Development  [x]

Unlike contracts and salary, biological time continues.

While provisional:

### Continue

* aging;
* growth;
* decay;
* hidden development trajectory;
* age-related development effects.

### Do not grant

* match participation development;
* competitive appearance bonuses;
* form bonuses based on fake/practice fixtures.

This means waiting has some natural player-development consequences without financially penalizing players for joining during the lockout period.

# 23. Promotion Rules  [x]

Every active non-bottom division normally relegates:

```text
2 clubs
```

Its two child divisions normally provide:

```text
1 promoted human each
```

Thus:

```text
2 relegated
2 promoted
```

---

# 24. AI Cannot Be Promoted  [x]

AI filler clubs are completely ineligible for promotion.

Promotion ranking operates on humans only.

Example standings:

```text
1 AI Alpha       31 pts
2 AI Beta        27 pts
3 Marcelo FC     25 pts
4 Human FC       22 pts
```

Promotion ranking is:

```text
#1 human = Marcelo FC
#2 human = Human FC
```

The AI clubs may visually occupy positions 1 and 2 but consume no promotion slots.

---

# 25. Promotion From Two Child Divisions  [x]

If both child divisions exist and contain humans:

```text
best human in child A → promoted
best human in child B → promoted
```

Example:

```text
2.1 winner human → Division 1
2.2 winner human → Division 1
```

---

# 26. Promotion When Only One Child Division Exists  [x]

Do not spawn an empty sibling division.

Example:

```text
2.1 exists
2.2 does not exist
```

Then the two available promotions to Division 1 come from:

```text
best human in 2.1
second-best human in 2.1
```

The same rule recursively applies:

```text
3.1 exists
3.2 doesn't
→ top 2 eligible humans from 3.1 can promote into 2.1
```

---

# 27. Possible Promotion Status  [x]

Before the join cutoff, the second promotion place may not yet be guaranteed because a sibling division could still spawn.

Example:

```text
2.1 exists
2.2 does not
join window remains open
```

Display:

```text
1st eligible human → PROMOTION
2nd eligible human → POSSIBLE PROMOTION
```

If `2.2` later spawns:

```text
2.1 best human → PROMOTION
2.2 best human → PROMOTION
```

If the join threshold closes without `2.2` spawning:

```text
2.1 top human → PROMOTION
2.1 second human → PROMOTION
```

The UI should update immediately after the lock.

# 29. Relegation  [x]

Normally:

```text
bottom 2 clubs in each non-bottom division are relegated
```

Human and AI-controlled historical clubs may be relegated.

Ordinary AI filler should exist only at the lowest active edge and therefore should generally not require downstream placement.

Do not try to permanently map:

```text
relegated #1 → child A
relegated #2 → child B
```

because divisions are rebuilt between seasons.

Instead determine the club's **new tier**, then regroup all clubs at that tier.

---

# 30. Tier Movement Is Persistent; Group Membership Is Not  [x]

At season end determine only:

```text
club.nextTier
```

Do not preserve:

```text
club.nextDivision = "2.1"
```

until regrouping.

Example:

```text
Club A: Tier 2
Club B: Tier 2
Club C: Tier 2
...
```

Then the clustering process generates new divisions.

This allows:

```text
2026-08: Club A in 2.1
2026-09: Club A in 2.2
```

without affecting its competitive status.

# 31. End-of-Season Reconstruction Pipeline  [x]

Run the following process transactionally or through an idempotent workflow.

## Phase 1 — Freeze season

Prevent mutations affecting final standings.

## Phase 2 — Finalize matches

Verify all 14 rounds completed.

## Phase 3 — Calculate final standings

Apply existing competition tie-breakers.

## Phase 4 — Identify abandoned clubs

Apply inactivity policy.

## Phase 5 — Remove abandoned clubs from next-season pyramid

Do not alter the completed season's historical table.

## Phase 6 — Determine relegations

Calculate normal tier movement.

## Phase 7 — Determine promotions

Ignore abandoned and AI clubs.

## Phase 8 — Fill vacancies

Promote additional eligible humans where abandoned clubs created additional vacancies.

## Phase 9 — Assign each persistent club a target tier

Example:

```text
clubId 123 → Tier 1
clubId 456 → Tier 2
```

## Phase 10 — Add provisional/returning clubs

Place eligible waiting clubs into the lowest available tier structure.

## Phase 11 — Rebuild divisions

Discard lower-tier group assignments.

## Phase 12 — Cluster humans

Maximize human density first, then optimize timezone.

## Phase 13 — Fill final partial division with AI

Only where required.

## Phase 14 — Delete obsolete divisions

Remove unused empty division entities.

## Phase 15 — Generate schedules

14 rounds per division.

## Phase 16 — Reset standings

All new-season clubs begin at:

```text
0 pts
0 W
0 D
0 L
0 GF
0 GA
```

## Phase 17 — Activate season

Change clubs and season to active state.

# 32. Vacancy Filling After Abandonment  [x]

Example Division 1:

```text
8 humans
3rd place = abandoned
7th = active
8th = active
```

Normally:

```text
2 clubs leave through relegation
2 clubs promote
```

But the abandoned club also leaves.

Therefore Division 1 has:

```text
3 vacancies
```

Promote 3 eligible humans from Tier 2.

---

## 32.1 Abandoned Club Already in Relegation Zone

Example:

```text
7th = abandoned
8th = active
```

Both leave Division 1 anyway.

Therefore:

```text
2 vacancies
```

Only two promotions are needed.

Do not count abandonment twice.

# 33. Extra Promotion Ranking  [x]

Where abandonment creates extra upward vacancies, select additional humans using a deterministic cross-division ranking.

Recommended hierarchy:

1. points;
2. goal difference;
3. goals scored;
4. wins;
5. existing competition tie-breaker;
6. deterministic final fallback.

Only compare clubs from the relevant immediate lower tier.

AI clubs are excluded.

Abandoned clubs are excluded.

---

# 34. Promotion Cascades  [x]

Extra promotions may create vacancies one tier lower.

Process top-down:

```text
Tier 1 vacancies
→ fill from Tier 2

Tier 2 vacancies
→ fill from Tier 3

Tier 3 vacancies
→ fill from Tier 4
```

Continue until:

* all higher-tier vacancies are filled; or
* there are no eligible humans below.

AI must never be promoted simply to fill a higher division.

If insufficient humans exist, evaluate the pyramid compaction rules before using AI.

---

# 35. Pyramid Compaction  [x]

Because AI should only fill the lowest active tier, the pyramid should compact at season rollover.

Example before rollover:

```text
Tier 1 = 8 humans
Tier 2 = 6 humans
Tier 3 = 2 humans
```

Do not necessarily preserve these tiers blindly if sporting promotion/relegation results and abandonment allow restructuring.

However:

> Never arbitrarily promote a club merely to eliminate AI if doing so violates competition results.

Competitive tier movement is determined first.

Grouping/compaction occurs **within each resulting tier**.

---

# 36. Timezone-Based Clustering  [x]

After target tiers are known, group clubs within each tier.

Priorities:

1. minimum number of divisions;
2. maximum human density;
3. minimum timezone differences.

Example:

```text
19 humans
```

Must produce:

```text
8
8
3 + 5 AI
```

Then determine which players belong to each 8-person cluster based on timezone.

---

## 36.1 Suggested Clustering Algorithm

For each tier:

1. Obtain all human clubs.
2. Convert IANA timezone into an appropriate representative local-time coordinate for grouping.
3. Sort roughly by longitude/timezone.
4. Partition into groups of 8.
5. Optimize boundary swaps to reduce internal timezone spread.
6. Add AI only to the final incomplete group.

Do not allow timezone optimization to create additional divisions.

---

## 36.2 DST

Calculate actual local offsets using IANA timezone rules when needed.

Never permanently save:

```text
timezoneOffset = -3
```

as the source of truth.

---

# 37. Match Timing  [x]

The architecture should support either:


# 47. Dormant Versus Provisional Economics  [x]

Treat these as separate states.

## Provisional

The club is actively waiting for its scheduled first/re-entry season.

Rules explicitly defined:

* salary frozen;
* contracts frozen;
* age continues;
* natural growth/decay continues.

## Dormant

The owner has abandoned the competition and has not returned.

Recommended implementation for the first multiplayer version:

* no league salary charges;
* contract countdown frozen;
* players continue aging;
* natural development/decay continues;
* no competitive development;
* no league income;
* no seasonal budget allocations.

This prevents dormant clubs from being destroyed financially while also preventing them from accumulating competition income.

This should remain a configurable economic policy because it may need balancing after observing real player behavior.

# 48. Returning From Dormancy  [x]

When a dormant club becomes provisional or active:

* contracts resume;
* salary obligations resume only when active;
* no retroactive salary is charged;
* no retroactive contract seasons are consumed;
* no missed seasonal budgets are awarded.

This prevents inactivity from becoming a way to bank months of budget.

# 54. Server Downtime Recovery  [x]

> "the scheduler happened to run at exactly 20:00."

* auctions still settle correctly after recovery;
* matches still process;
* season state catches up.

# 92. Core Design Invariants  [x]

These should be documented directly in the codebase.

1. **Every active division always contains exactly 8 competition slots.**
2. **A season is globally synchronized.**
3. **Every club plays at most one division per season.**
4. **AI filler can never be promoted.**
5. **Human density takes priority over timezone clustering.**
6. **Empty divisions do not persist unnecessarily.**
7. **Division group membership is disposable between seasons.**
8. **Tier is the meaningful competitive level.**
9. **No new human enters the current season after the configured join lock.**
10. **Post-lock clubs remain playable through provisional preparation.**
11. **A provisional club receives its upcoming season's budget only once.**
12. **Provisional players age and naturally develop/decay.**
13. **Provisional contracts do not elapse.**
14. **Provisional salaries are not charged.**
15. **Practice matches cannot farm persistent progression.**
16. **Abandoned clubs are never removed mid-season.**
17. **Abandoned clubs retain their underlying club, squad, finances and progression.**
18. **Returning abandoned clubs re-enter at the lowest available competitive level.**
19. **Completed fixture results are immutable.**
20. **Scheduled multiplayer operations must be idempotent and recoverable after downtime.**

# 93. Target Example  [x]

Assume the game eventually has:

```text
Tier 1: 8 humans

Tier 2:
2.1: 8 humans
2.2: 8 humans

Tier 3:
3.1: 8 humans
3.2: 8 humans
3.3: 5 humans + 3 AI
3.4: nonexistent
```

During the season:

* no AI above `3.3`;
* AI in `3.3` cannot promote;
* humans may replace those AI until join lock;
* if another 3 humans join, `3.3` becomes 8 humans;
* the next human requires creation of `3.4`;
* `3.4` is simulated through the current round before the human enters;
* after the join lock, further users become provisional.

At season end:

1. abandonment is evaluated;
2. promotion/relegation is calculated;
3. additional promotions fill abandonment vacancies;
4. all `.1/.2/.3/.4` memberships are discarded;
5. humans receive target tiers;
6. Tier 2 and Tier 3 are regrouped primarily by timezone;
7. groups are packed 8-at-a-time;
8. AI fills only the final incomplete bottom division;
9. new schedules are generated;
10. standings reset;
11. provisional clubs become active;
12. salaries/contracts begin or resume for those newly active clubs;
13. the new month begins simultaneously across the game.

This should be the foundation of the multiplayer league architecture.

## Addendum (2026-08-20): review fixes affecting multiplayer

Implemented per `code-review-2026-08-20.md`:

* **New-club starting cash is zero** (`MP_CONFIG.newClubStartingCash`); the
  season allocation is the only initial funding (§17A unchanged otherwise).
* **New-club sell lock** (`MP_CONFIG.newClubSellLockMatches = 3`): a human club
  cannot list players for transfer auction or loan until it has played that
  many of its OWN league fixtures, counted from played fixtures involving the
  club — never from inherited standings rows (a mid-season joiner inherits the
  replaced AI's record but none of its played matches). Filler AI is exempt.
  This amends §10-style provisional freedoms: buying players and releasing
  players stay enabled; outbound markets are gated (INVARIANTS #10/#22).
* **Live matches advance only on the server clock** (§37/§54 pacing): client
  tick/finish acceleration paths are removed; the worker's real-time pacing is
  authoritative and a match lasts exactly `MP_CONFIG.matchDurationMinutes`.
* **Stadium/ticket/gate mechanics removed**: gate receipts no longer exist as
  income; the season budget (§17A) is the primary funding source and its
  formula is intentionally unchanged.
